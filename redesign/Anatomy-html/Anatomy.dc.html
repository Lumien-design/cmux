Colour is one channel and it belongs to attention.
Blue means a pane is blocked on you — nothing else. Green means a process is healthy. Red means it failed. Selection gets no colour at all: it gets a surface lift and a neutral bar, because you already know where you are.
The elapsed timer is the feature.
With eight agents running, the only question that orders your next action is which one has been stuck longest. Put it on every row, right-aligned, tabular, and sort by it.
Demote the path, promote the agent.
The path is the longest string and the least discriminating — it is already in the title bar. Which agent is driving the workspace is not shown anywhere today.
Two lines, never five.
Line one is the scan line: state, name, clock. Line two is the context line, and it is muted so it never competes.
