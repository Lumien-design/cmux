Collapsed rail & keyboard model
Collapsing should cost metadata, not awareness

44px. One pip per workspace in sort order, same glyph and colour as the full row. Selection keeps its neutral bar. The count at the foot is how many are blocked.
⌘1Jump to the Nth visible row. Hold ⌘ to reveal the index caps.
⌥⇥Next workspace blocked on you, longest wait first. The one shortcut that matters.
⌥⇧⇥Previous blocked workspace.
⌘KFilter. Matches name, branch, path, agent and port.
↑↓Move focus without switching. ↵ commits, ⎋ restores.
→Expand the workspace into its panes. ← collapses.
⌘BCollapse to the rail. State survives — the pips keep reporting.
⌘⌃PPin a workspace above the sort so it never moves.
The list is a listbox, not a stack of divs.
Roving tabindex, role="listbox" on the container, role="option" with aria-selected on each row, and the group headers as role="group" with aria-label. That is what makes ↑↓ and type-ahead free rather than hand-rolled.
Announce the transition, not the tick.
A row entering “needs input” is the only thing worth an aria-live="polite" announcement: “fix-ssh needs input.” The elapsed timer must be aria-hidden or a screen reader recites the clock every second.
Under prefers-reduced-motion.
The blocked pip stops pulsing and stays at full opacity — the resting state of the loop, never the dim end. Nothing else in the sidebar moves.
