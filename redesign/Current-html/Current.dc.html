Audit
What the shipped sidebar gets wrong
Colours sampled from the screenshot; contrast computed against WCAG 2.1 relative luminance.
1
Every line on the selected row fails WCAG AA.
Measured from the screenshot: the fill is #3F8FF7. White title on it is 3.24:1 — under the 4.5:1 floor for normal text. #D9E9FD is 2.63:1; the branch and path at #CFE3FD are 2.48:1. The marketing mock's darker --t-selected #0A5BB5 clears it at 6.61 / 5.23 — the shipped app is less accessible than its own mockup.
2
Selection and attention are the same channel.
The row is blue because it is selected. It is also the row that needs you. You cannot tell which meaning the blue carries, and the moment a second workspace starts waiting the encoding has nothing left to say it with.
3
The same fact is stated three times.
“Claude is waiting for your input”, the bell, and “Needs input” are one bit of information spending three lines. Meanwhile how long it has been waiting — the thing that actually orders your attention across N agents — is nowhere.
4
Five lines per workspace does not scale.
At ~92px a row, ten agents overflow a 1080p sidebar. cmux exists to run agents in parallel; the row is sized for one.
5
The row answers the wrong questions.
It spends its two widest lines on develop and ~/Repos/cmux/cmux-tui — both already visible in the tab bar and title. Missing: which agent, elapsed, diff size, how many panes and how many of them are blocked.
6
The workspace has two panes. The sidebar says nothing.
* cmux is blocked and ◇ Ready (cmux-tui) is idle. The unit of attention is the pane; the list is only modelling workspaces.
7
85% of the column is dead space.
No sort, no filter, no grouping, no keyboard index, no empty state doing any work.
8
The column has no edge.
Sidebar #222324 against terminal #1E1E1E is 1.06:1 and there is no rule between them. The panel reads as a floating card on nothing.
